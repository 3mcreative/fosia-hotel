Welcome to Fosia Hotel

Who We Are ?

Derived from the Sanskrit word “Fortuna,” meaning “luck” or “fortune,” FOSIA embodies a commitment to emotional well-being and prosperity. Established in 2023, FOSIA operates as a distinguished holding company within the hospitality sector, featuring multiple esteemed hotel brands. Guided by the motto “GROW THROUGH INNOVATION,” FOSIA strives to redefine guest experiences through groundbreaking innovations in service, facilities, interior design, and event offerings, ensuring each stay is uniquely memorable and enriching.

To become a national hotel management company that provides comfort through continuous innovation and creativity

FortunaGrande Malioboro

Hotel FortunaGrande Malioboro Yogyakarta stands prominently in the heart of Malioboro’s bustling shopping district. An esteemed destination for both local and international travelers, this area is renowned for its immaculate pedestrian avenues, ideal for leisurely strolls and capturing memorable moments through photography. It serves as a veritable haven for shopping enthusiasts, offering a diverse array of fashion and culinary delights. Our hotel boasts a modern, minimalist interior design, complemented by serene pockets of natural greenery throughout the premises. We are dedicated to providing you with a tranquil and luxurious lodging experience, ensuring your comfort and enjoyment throughout your stay. ELEGANT AND COMFORTABLE ACCOMMODATIONS Conveniently located just a brief 5-minute walk from Malioboro Street, Hotel FortunaGrande Malioboro features 98 rooms across four distinct types, tailored to the needs of both leisure and business travelers alike. Enhanced with middle-scale amenities, including a rooftop swimming pool with breathtaking views, a chic lounge area, a cozy coffee shop, and well-appointed meeting rooms, our hotel promises a refined and memorable stay for every guest.
